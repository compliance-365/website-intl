What buyers in the US ask for

ISO 42001, implemented on Microsoft

Your AI inventory, impact assessments and oversight records sit next to the Microsoft 365, Entra and Defender data they describe, so the AI management system is evidenced from the tenant rather than rebuilt in a separate tool.

An ISO 27001 Statement of Applicability you can defend

Every Annex A control with its justification, owner, status and a link to its evidence, with the technical controls checked against your live tenant configuration.

SOC 2 from enterprise buyers

US enterprise security reviews usually ask for a SOC 2 report. We get you ready for Type I and the Type II observation period. The report itself is issued by an independent CPA firm, which you engage directly.

Compliance checks that run themselves

Posture checks read your Microsoft 365 and Azure configuration, and an optional scheduled monitor flags drift, so evidence stays current between audits instead of being gathered the week before.
